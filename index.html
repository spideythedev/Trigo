Device API
Hardware at Your Fingertips
Access sensors, radios, and system features directly from the browser. Trigo.js handles permissions, fallbacks, and cross-platform differences automatically.
📳

Vibrate patterns, haptic feedback, SOS, heartbeat rhythms. iOS haptics supported.
🔦
Flashlight
Toggle, blink, strobe, morse code, SOS, breathing effect, countdown.
📸
Camera & Mic
Open camera, capture photos, record video/audio, scan barcodes, measure mic level.
📍
Geolocation
Get position, watch movement, calculate speed, heading, distance between points.
📋
Clipboard & Share
Copy/paste text, HTML, images. Native share sheet for URL, text, and files.
🔔
Notifications
Push notifications with icons, actions, scheduling, badges, silent mode.
🖥️
Fullscreen & Wake Lock
Enter/exit fullscreen, keep screen awake, prevent device sleep.

NFC & Bluetooth
Read/write NFC tags, scan and connect Bluetooth devices, send/receive data.
Visual Effects
Beautiful Animations, One Line Each
Fade, slide, zoom, shake, pulse, glow, spin, bounce, holographic 3D effects, typewriter, scramble, gradient animations, and more. All with full customization.
🌫️
Fade & Blink
Fade in/out with custom opacity, duration, easing. Blink and flash with configurable intervals.
💫
Pulse & Glow
Scale pulse with configurable intensity. Box-shadow glow with custom colors and spread.
🪨
Shake & Bounce
Shake horizontally/vertically with custom intensity. Bounce with configurable height and count.
📐
Slide & Zoom
Slide from any direction. Zoom in/out from any origin. Custom distance and easing.
🔄
Spin & Reveal
Continuous spin with custom speed. Scroll-triggered reveal animations.
🌈
Holographic & 3D
Tilt-responsive holographic effect with glare and color shift. 3D perspective transforms.
⌨️
Typewriter & Scramble
Type text character by character with cursor. Scramble text reveal effect.
🎨
Gradient & Parallax
Animated color gradients. Scroll-based parallax effects on any element.
